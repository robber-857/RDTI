What counts as software R&D?+

Where a technical outcome is uncertain, we can design an experiment, test a hypothesis and record what happened. Routine integrations, marketing and day-to-day operations generally fall outside core R&D. Your adviser assesses eligibility activity by activity.

How we deliver

The same team from experiment to rollout.

Your developers test the hardest technical question, connect the system to your business and hand over the evidence for your adviser.

  1. Scope the business problem

    Pick a costly task, agree what better looks like and put a price on a first release. For a larger rollout, map the teams, systems and approvals involved.

  2. Test the technical risk

    Run a pilot on representative data. Log hypotheses, test results, time and costs as the engineers work, so your adviser can review the evidence.

  3. Deploy and measure

    Integrate the system, train your team and compare the result with the baseline you set together, from handling time to the cost of running each model.
